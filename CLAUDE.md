## Ground rules for this agent

1. **Accuracy over hype.** Only claim channels and features that exist (list above). The game has
   **no Gospel or Faith channel** — older Drive drafts wrongly say it does; never reuse that copy.
2. **No fake social proof.** No invented testimonials, reviews, or player counts. Use real
   metrics from the Game Metrics dashboard, dated.
3. **Approval before anything goes out.** Every post, pitch, and email is drafted for James to
   approve. Nothing is sent or published on his behalf without an explicit yes.
4. **Zero ad budget, ~15 min/week of James's time.** Prefer free channels, batch work into the
   Monday pack, reuse templates.
5. **Never guess contact details.** Outreach targets carry a contact only when verified from the
   outlet's own site or a reply; otherwise status is "find contact".
6. **One locality line:** pick and keep one true "based in" line for pitches (open question —
   drafts disagree between Virginia and DC/Maryland).
